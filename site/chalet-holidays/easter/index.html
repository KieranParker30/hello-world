Easter spring skiing

Longer days, a higher sun and a chalet balcony that's even more enticing — G&T, anyone? Some think the season's over by April, but Easter often brings big snowfalls and some of the best conditions of the year.

On this page
  1. Why Easter with Le Ski
  2. Sunday 28 March 2027
  3. Sunday 4 April 2027

Why Easter with Le Ski

  • Two Easters in a row with fantastic spring snow.
  • Perfect for children — they last much longer on the slopes on warm, sunny days than in mid-January.
  • Powder still falls — April showers in the Alps come down as snow.
  • Your last chance of the season if you're tied to school holidays. Call 01484 548996.
