Chalet Mire

Val d'Isère Sleeps 6 3 bedrooms
Availability

Stunning top quality chalet with beautiful views.

We are so pleased to announce yet another new property in Val d'Isere, Chalet Mire. It is the brainchild of the same family who built our most beautiful chalets, La Belle Etoile and Azel. Mire lies below Belle Etoile, near the eponymous river Isere, and is a very short walk from the bus stop which will take you either to La Daille or the Bellevarde Express.

Chalet Mire has been finished to the highest possible standards. The whole family have worked on the chalet themselves and they've also designed and built it in an environmentally friendly fashion - as far as is possible in the Alps.

The trees which had to be chopped down to make way for the chalet have been used throughout the building - some are window sills, some are exterior cladding, etc. There is an air source heat pump which uses already heated air to help heat the water. The family also plant new trees every year to replace those felled. All in all it's a pretty cool chalet!

The enormous and extremely comfortable lounge and dining areas have a welcoming log fireplace and huge picture windows on 3 sides as well as a large terrace. Everything about Chalet Mire is top quality, from the triple glazing to the fixtures and fittings to the beds and pillows.

All the bedrooms are spacious and have their own ensuite bathroom with walk in shower and separate wc. There is a hot tub and sauna in the ground floor spa area and a heated boot room and a ski store.

Chalet Mire is just a 2 minute walk to the nearest bus stop which takes you to the main lifts.
