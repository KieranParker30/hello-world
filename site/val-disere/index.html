Why we love it

Val d'Isère

The drive up is part of the magic: through the tunnels, past the Grande Motte glacier and the turquoise Lac du Chevril, before the valley opens out beneath the Bellevarde and Solaise peaks. Once thought too remote to work, it's now one of the most famous ski towns in the world.

If you have the stamina to ski all day and stay out late, Val is your place — from chic cocktail bars to live bands. Families are just as well served, with ice-skating, the Aquasportif centre and the Village Perdu kids' area. Free buses run end to end all day, so getting about is easy.

The skiing

Something for every level

Piste maps

01

Beginners

Warm up on the free Village drag, then head for the gentle Ski Tranquille area around Le Borsat.

02

Intermediates

Long reds like Criterium in La Daille, tree runs at Mattis when it snows, and the whole of Tignes a few lifts away.

03

Experts

La Face — the 1992 Olympic downhill — at the end of the day, and some of the most famous off-piste on the planet. Take a guide.

Off the slopes

Not everyone wants to ski every day

Ice-skating, the Aquasportif pools and spa, snowshoeing, dog-sledding and a patisserie whose award-winning window displays are worth the walk on their own.
