Chalet Eterlou

Courchevel Sleeps 4-6 2 bedrooms
Availability

Perfect for larger families - can easily accommodate 6 guests.

Chalet L'Eterlou is an extremely spacious and comfortable chalet sleeping four to six guests.

It offers particularly excellent value for a larger family seeking the perfect private chalet (see below for full details).

L'Eterlou is an exceptionally smart chalet in our stunning property 'Scalottas Lodge' which has beautiful views of the peaks of Courchevel 1850 and offers catered Le Ski chalets for 2-6 guests.

The chalet itself sleeps four as standard, but is extremely spacious and can easily accommodate a total of six in its two bedrooms. There is one large ensuite twin for 2 guests and one room with 2 sets of full size, specially commissioned, oak bunks which can sleep 4 and has its own private bathroom. Guests in this room receive £100 off per person from full price holidays.

Chalet L'Eterlou can be used in conjunction with any other chalet in the Lodge for larger groups. Please note that to enjoy watching your favourite shows on the TV here in this chalet please bring an HDMI cable to fit your device, an Amazon Fire stick or anything else.

Where you'll be

The Courchevel valley is four very different villages: Courchevel (1850), Moriond (1650), Village (1550) and Le Praz (1300). Our chalets are in Moriond — the friendliest of the four, with fewer queues, quieter pistes and the best sunsets in the valley.
