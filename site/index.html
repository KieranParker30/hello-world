One price, all of this

Everything taken care of, door to door

Our prices include flights, transfers, catering and wine. No hidden charges, no tiered service levels — what we describe is what you get. What's included

Our own Sunday flights

Gatwick or Manchester to Chambéry with 22kg hold bag included — the closest airport to the Alps, on the quietest day to travel.

Direct transfers

Our own coaches meet you at arrivals. La Tania in 80 minutes, Courchevel in 90, Val d'Isère and Tignes in two hours.

Proper chalet catering

Cooked breakfast, afternoon tea and a three-course dinner five nights a week, made fresh by your chalet host.

Wine with dinner

Unlimited house wine at dinner, with coffee and mints, plus a cheeseboard once a week.

Champagne & canapés

Champagne and canapés on your first and last nights, and local Méthode Champenoise on the evenings between.

A team in resort

A Guest Services Manager to book restaurants, lift passes, lessons and hire — so you don't have to.
