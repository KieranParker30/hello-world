Why we love it

Tignes Les Boisses (1800)

Tignes is a handful of villages: Val Claret, Le Lac, Le Lavachet, Les Boisses (1800) and Les Brevières. We're in Les Boisses, on the far side of the dam beside the replica of the church lost when the valley was flooded in 1952.

Since 2013, investment has turned the hamlet into a ski-in, ski-out village with its own shops and restaurants, and regular buses link it to the rest of Tignes. It's the perfect base to explore the tree runs down to Les Brevières and get a selfie at the Aiguille Percée.
