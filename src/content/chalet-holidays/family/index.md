## Mealtimes that work for children

Afternoon tea is waiting when you get back from the slopes. In the evening we're happy to serve an early children's supper — or move dinner for the whole family — and our hosts can cook a simpler children's menu. Just let our UK team know before you travel.

## Courchevel & La Tania with children

### On the slopes

Courchevel 1650 and La Tania are relatively quiet, with plenty of gentle greens and blues and dedicated beginner areas served by magic carpets — a safe place for children to learn.

Courchevel 1650 has the themed Western Park, and 1850 has a Family Park with jumps, ski-cross and a big air bag, plus an Adventure Camp at the Altiport. There's a small sledging area at the top of the Tovets chairlift, and older children will love the 3km **Moriond Racing** luge track, with twists, turns and tunnels through the trees.

### Off the slopes

[Aquamotion](aquamotion) in Courchevel is Europe's largest mountain aqua centre: indoor and outdoor pools, a baby pool, slides, diving boards and a surf pool. In Courchevel 1850, the Forum has ice-skating, climbing, bowling and a cinema, and La Croisette has soft play for younger children.

### Nannies and nurseries

- **Courchevel Nanny Services** cover both resorts — mention Le Ski when booking.
- **ESF** run a specialist nursery (6 months–6 years) and Club Piou (3–12 years).
- **Fabienne**, an English-speaking French nanny with years of private childcare experience, covers both resorts: +33 687 866 053.

## Val d'Isère with children

### On the slopes

There are two nursery areas — in the village centre and at La Daille. The main one, at the bottom of the Solaise lift, has three free lifts and gentle greens, with a second beginner area at the top of Solaise. **Val Kids** zones on Solaise and Bellevarde have a playground, games piste, toboggan run and mini ski-cross. There's a sledging area beside the Savonette nursery lift.

### Off the slopes

The **Aquasportif** centre has a pool with river currents and water cannons, plus a climbing wall. Take the bus to the **Ferme de l'Androit** in the Manchet valley to meet cows, horses and pigs, and buy local produce at milking time. There's also an outdoor ice rink in the village, open into the evening.

### Nannies and nurseries

- **Crèche du Village des Enfants** — 6 to 36 months, with a kindergarten for 3 to 13 years.
- **Oxygène** run lunch clubs and afternoon kids' clubs: treasure hunts, igloo building, swimming, climbing, sledging and snowshoeing.
- **Boubird** childcare are very flexible — mention Le Ski for better rates.
