## New Generation

**Courchevel, Val d'Isère and La Tania**

We're delighted to recommend New Generation in all three resorts. Their English-speaking coaches consistently get excellent results and feedback.

| Group | Ages | Hours | Group size | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kids' & teen groups | 6–17 | 4 hrs a day | 2–8 | from €550 a week |
| Whippersnappers | 4–5 | 2 hrs a day | 2–6 | €350 a week |

Outside school holidays, children's groups run on request — contact New Generation directly.
