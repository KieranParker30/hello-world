## How it works

Unlimited house red, white or rosé is included with every dinner. To upgrade, call us on 01484 548996 and we'll add the Wine Lovers' Package to your booking. We send you a simple order form, and your wines will be waiting when you arrive. *Santé.*

Our wine taster has spent many hours choosing these for quality and value — you may not know them all, but that's half the fun.
