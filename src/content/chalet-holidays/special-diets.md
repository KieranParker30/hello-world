## How we handle it

We're massive foodies and proud of what we serve. Specialist products aren't as easy to find in the Alps as in the UK, so please tell us your requirements **in detail when you book**. The more we know, the better we can plan; if you wait until you arrive, it can take several days to source a product.

For gluten-free guests we provide a cake on arrival and a gluten-free option every day (though you may see the same cake twice in a week).

We'll also try to work around foods you really can't abide. If you're simply "not keen", that's trickier. And we can't cater for a holiday diet experiment — for that, we'd suggest a health spa!

## What we can provide

- Gluten-free flour, pasta, bread, cakes and cereals
- Dairy-free spread and soya yoghurts
- Soya, goat, almond, rice and oat milks
- Quinoa and other grains and pulses
- Tofu, Quorn and other meat substitutes

## What we can't provide

- Rice or other speciality flours
- Specific organic items
- Manufactured "free-from" products, including sugar-free and vegan-labelled items

::: callout
**Pack your favourites.** If something you rely on isn't on the list above, bring it with you. The list isn't exhaustive — you may use something we haven't heard of yet. Questions? Call 01484 548996.
:::
