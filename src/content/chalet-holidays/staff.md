## Guest Services Team

Your first point of contact at Chambéry airport. They'll make the move from plane to coach to chalet smooth and stress-free.

## Chalet hosts

Your host makes the chalet home for the week — cooking breakfast, baking afternoon tea and serving three-course dinners, as well as looking after the housekeeping. They're flexible, professional and genuinely care about their chalet and their guests. See [chalet food](chalet-holidays/food).

## Guest Services Managers

Every resort has a Guest Services Manager whose job is simply to make sure you're having a great holiday. They'll pop in at breakfast or in the evening, can be reached 24/7 if something's wrong, and are happy to book restaurants, ski school, taxis and more.

## Behind the scenes

Chalet managers, resort support and maintenance teams work hard to keep everything running smoothly. You may never see them, and that's rather the point.
