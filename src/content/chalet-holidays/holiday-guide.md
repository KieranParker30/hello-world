## Passports

Check your passport's validity. British passport holders may need at least three months left to travel to France, and the rules are changing — see [GOV.UK foreign travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/france/entry-requirements). You won't need a visa for a holiday (only for stays over 90 days in 180). If you're not a British or EU passport holder, please check visa requirements. See also the new [EU Entry/Exit System](chalet-holidays/flights#the-eu-entry-exit-system-ees).

## Insurance

- **GHIC** — UK citizens should carry a Global Health Insurance Card (formerly EHIC) for free or discounted state medical treatment in the EU. [Apply on the NHS website](https://www.nhs.uk/using-the-nhs/healthcare-abroad/apply-for-a-free-uk-global-health-insurance-card-ghic/).
- **Winter sports insurance** is a condition of booking. See our [insurance guidance](chalet-holidays/insurance).
- **Carré Neige** can be added to your lift pass in resort, covering mountain rescue.

Take copies of your policy, including its emergency procedures. In France the rule is pay now, claim later — keep every receipt from doctors, hospitals and pharmacies. Your Guest Services Manager is there to help if anything goes wrong.

## Getting there

At Chambéry our Guest Services team will meet you and travel with you on the coach to resort.

| | Terminal | Parking |
|---|---|---|
| Gatwick | South Terminal | Book in advance online |
| Manchester | Terminal 2 | Quote "Le Ski" for special prices |

Transfers take 80 minutes to La Tania, 90 minutes to Courchevel and two hours to Val d'Isère and Tignes. Driving? Please call our French office (+33 (0)4 79 08 19 39) when you're about 30 minutes away. See [flights & transfers](chalet-holidays/flights) for baggage and ski carriage, and our [green policy](chalet-holidays/green-policies) for carbon offsetting.

## When you arrive

Your chalet host will meet you in resort, show you to your chalet and put the kettle on — there'll be a freshly baked cake waiting. Your Guest Services Manager will visit during the week to answer questions, book activities and reserve restaurants.
