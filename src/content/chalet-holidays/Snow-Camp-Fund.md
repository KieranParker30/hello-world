## How you can help

We ask guests to add £2, £5 or £10 to their booking for the Snow-Camp fund. Every penny goes straight to the charity — no admin charges. Huge thanks to everyone who has already contributed.

## What we do

Since 2015 we've donated a Le Ski holiday for two to Snow-Camp every year. It's auctioned at their fundraising events and raises a large sum for young people. Read [the Snow-Camp story](https://www.snow-camp.org.uk/).

We've also helped other charities, including the Teenage Cancer Trust, by donating a holiday for two at a Yorkshire fundraiser each year. This year's chosen charity is **Admiral Nurses — Dementia UK**, with local legends Razorbach playing the Holmfirth Picturedrome in October.
