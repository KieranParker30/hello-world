## Included in your price

- **Expert advice** from our UK team, who'll help you choose the right chalet for your group.
- **Return flights** from London Gatwick or Manchester to Chambéry, with a 22kg hold bag each. Manchester is £60 extra; see [Flights & transfers](chalet-holidays/flights).
- **Return transfers** from Chambéry to your chalet on our own coaches when you fly with us.
- **Chalet board**: cooked breakfast, home-baked afternoon tea and a three-course dinner with unlimited wine, coffee and mints, five days a week.
- **Champagne and canapés** on your first and last evenings, with canapés and local Méthode Champenoise on the other catered nights. A cheeseboard once a week.
- **Your chalet host** to make the chalet home for the week, and a **Guest Services Manager** on call 24/7 to book restaurants, lessons and passes.
- **Towels changed midweek**, plus robes and slippers in chalets with hot tubs. Hairdryer and UK extension lead in every bedroom.
- **WiFi** in every chalet at no extra charge.
- **Taxes**: French tourist tax and VAT (about £50) and UK and EU airport and security taxes (about £50).
- **100% financial protection** through ATOL and ABTOT — see [Is my money safe?](chalet-holidays/bonding)

## Empty bed charges

If a bedroom isn't fully occupied there's a charge of 50% of the standard price for each empty bed. There's a limit to how many empty beds each chalet can carry, so please call us on 01484 548996 if your group is an odd number.

## Five days' catering, less plastic

Like other chalet operators, we cater five days a week. For Sunday departures your host's days off are usually Tuesday and Friday; we lay out a continental breakfast and afternoon tea, and our Guest Services Manager will book you somewhere good for dinner.

We removed 10,000 small shower gel and shampoo bottles a season by switching to large dispensers of body wash in every bathroom, plus a bar of soap at the basin.
