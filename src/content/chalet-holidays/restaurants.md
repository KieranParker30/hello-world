### Méribel, St Martin & Val Thorens

- **La Folie Douce** (Méribel, Saulire Express) — terrace dining and snacks; great for non-skiers. +33 4 79 06 07 17
- **L'Adray Télébar**, **Lodge du Village**, **Maya Altitude** and **La Sittelle** (Méribel) — great terraces and consistently good food.
- **Le Corbeleys** (St Martin) — cosy farm building with a sunny terrace. +33 4 79 08 95 31
- **L'eski's** (St Martin — no relation!) — staff favourite for value and sun. **Le Montagnard** — lovely terrace.
- **Chez Pépé Nicolas** (Val Thorens) — local produce and views. **La Ferme de Reberty** — the perfect March lunch; book on the day. **Le Grand Lac** — the best French onion soup.
