## Courchevel

### Courchevel Moriond (1650)

- **Fire and Ice** — sunny terrace, or sofas, blankets and log fires inside.
- **Copina** — cocktails, craft beer, world wines and tapas; popular with locals.
- **Le Schuss** — mulled wine, hot chocolate, or a La Chouffe if you fancy something stronger.
- **Le Funky Fox** — games, sports screens, good music and reasonable prices.
- **Pelican** — lively, with live music during the week and bar food.

### Courchevel 1850, Le Praz & Méribel

- **La Plante de Bâton** (1850) — on the piste front, catching the last of the sun with a live DJ.
- **Le P'tit Drink** (1850) — cosy wine and tapas bar for a quiet, classy evening.
- **L'Eléphant** (Le Praz) — sports bar with pool and darts; live music at après; family-friendly.
- **Les Peupliers** (Le Praz) — fireside drinks or a sunny terrace overlooking the lake.
- **La Folie Douce** (Méribel) — a dance floor with a view.
- **Le Rond Point** (Méribel) — one of the best après bars in Europe, with live music and sunset views.

## Courchevel La Tania

- **Pub Le Ski Lodge** — big sun terrace, sports screens and a buzzy atmosphere.
- **La Taïga** — cosy cocktail bar beneath the restaurant, with regular live music. Arrive early.

## Val d'Isère

| Bar | Why go |
|---|---|
| **Cocorico** | Party on the piste with live music and DJ sets at sunset, above Doudoune |
| **La Folie Douce** | The original — cabaret, DJs and Champagne. Dancing in ski boots is easier than it sounds |
| **Rose Blanche** | Bottom of the La Daille gondola, great in the sun |
| **Le Petit Danois** | Pool and live music at après, then a club till late |
| **Bananas** | Two bars: one for beer, one for spirits. A clever way not to mix your drinks |
| **Blue Note** | Locals' favourite — table football, pool, darts and happy hour |
| **Le Hibou** | Good-value cocktails a short walk from our Cacholet chalets |
| **Le XV** | French bar with a great atmosphere for watching sport |
| **Victor's** | Chic Scandinavian cocktail bar and restaurant |
| **Dick's Tea Bar** | Legendary club, open until the early hours. Go at least once |
| **La Baraque** | Sophisticated, with live bands and late-night nibbles |
| **Le Garage** | Busy, with live music, entertainment and sports |

## Tignes

- **Bar Ô Cav Ô** (Les Boisses 1800) — friendly local bar by the piste and shops.
- **Escale Blanche** (Le Lac) — central, with a massive terrace and great staff.
- **Loop Bar** (Le Lac) — British-run, by the piste and beginners' area.
- **L'Embuscade** (Le Lac) — lively, in the Palafour building.
- **Jam Bar** (Le Lac) — small, family-run, near the lifts.
- **The Marmot Arms** (Le Lac) — friendly, with occasional live music.
- **Taverne des Neiges** (Val Claret) — at the foot of the Grande Motte.
