## New Generation

**Courchevel, Val d'Isère, La Tania and Tignes**

We recommend New Generation in all four resorts. Their English-speaking coaches get excellent results and feedback. Prices vary by date and resort:

| Lesson | Price |
|---|---|
| Group course — 5 × 2 hours, max 8 per class | €300 |
| Private — 2 hours, up to 2 people | from €155 |
| Private — 2.5 hours, up to 2 people | €180–190 |
| Private — 4 hours, up to 2 people | €295–375 |
| Private — 6 hours, 1–3 people | €500 |

Longer children's lessons run in school holidays — call New Generation on 01462 674000 or see our [children's ski school guide](chalet-holidays/family/ski-school-children).
