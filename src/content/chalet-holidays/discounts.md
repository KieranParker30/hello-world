## Child discounts

Based on age on the departure date.

| Age | Sharing parents' room | Own room in La Tania | Own room in Courchevel, Tignes or Val d'Isère |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 2 | £200, including cot and highchair | — | — |
| 2–4 | 50% off | 20% off | 15% off |
| 5–12 | 35% off | 15% off | 10% off |

Chalet Le Rabel also has family room discounts — call us for details. More on [family holidays](chalet-holidays/family).

## Loyalty discount

We're lucky to have such loyal guests — thank you. If this is your **third holiday with us in the last five seasons**, you'll get **10% off**.

Loyalty discounts apply to full-price bookings only, and can't be combined with extra-bed discounts of more than 20%.
