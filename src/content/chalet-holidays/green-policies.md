## What Le Ski is doing

- **Paperless** — all our UK booking systems are now paperless.
- **Sustainable menus** — adapted to reduce waste and carbon footprint. Ask your host for details.
- **Less plastic** — we removed 10,000 small toiletry bottles a season with large body-wash dispensers.
- **Electric shuttles** — our Val d'Isère shuttle uses electric vehicles, charged on site.
- **Surplus food donated** at the end of each season, to locals and charities including Care for Calais and the Convoi d'Aide Alpin to Ukraine.
- **Staff training** on the local environment, plus waste separation in our kitchens.
- **Smart heating** — remotely controlled thermostats in our Val d'Isère Cacholet chalets.
- **Community** — our teams join One Tree at a Time's "Fix It" kit-repair events and "Climb it for Climate" hikes.

## Before you travel

### Check your kit

Before buying new: **repair** what you have; **rent** (try Eco-Ski or Ski Chic); or **buy and sell second-hand** on WhoSki, Vinted or eBay. In Les 3 Vallées, One Tree at a Time sells repurposed kit from well-known brands.

### Offset your flight

A return flight to Chambéry produces roughly **0.36 tonnes of CO₂ from Gatwick** and **0.44 tonnes from Manchester**. We suggest donating £10 to One Tree at a Time, a local Alpine environmental charity, or to the [Woodland Trust](https://www.woodlandtrust.org.uk/) in the UK.

### Pack light

We provide towels and hairdryers. Bring a refillable water bottle and plastic-free toiletries like shampoo bars, and consider public transport to the airport.

## In resort

- **Learn about your surroundings** — the resort information folder in your chalet covers local geography, wildlife and culture.
- **Recycle** — follow the notices in your chalet, or ask your host.
- **Take everything home** — unwanted clothing and kit puts pressure on local waste systems.
- **Turn off lights** when you go out, and **don't litter** on the mountain — it all reappears when the snow melts.
