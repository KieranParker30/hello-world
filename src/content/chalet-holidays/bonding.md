## What "covered" means

| If Le Ski failed… | Flight-inclusive holiday (ATOL) | Holiday without flights (ABTOT) |
|---|---|---|
| …before you travelled | The CAA refunds what you've paid | ABTOT refunds what you've paid |
| …while you were away | The CAA gets you home | ABTOT gets you home |

## You don't need a credit card

It doesn't matter how you pay — you're covered by our ATOL or ABTOT bonds.

## Why it matters

Guests affected by Covid closures soon learned how important bonding is. Many unbonded operators simply ignored their customers and never refunded them. As an ATOL-bonded operator we're held to firm standards, and we pay the CAA to insure every guest who books a holiday with flights.

::: callout
**Comparing operators?** Make sure they're properly bonded. Some display an ATOL logo but don't actually provide flights — so you're not covered. If in doubt, ask, or check the [CAA's ATOL register](https://www.atol.org/about-atol/what-does-atol-protection-mean/).
:::

## ABTOT details

ABTOT (the Association of Bonded Travel Organisers Trust) provides financial protection for Le Ski Ltd under the Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018: a refund if you haven't yet travelled, or repatriation if you're abroad. Bookings made outside the EU are only protected when purchased directly with Le Ski Ltd.

If you need help abroad because of financial failure, call ABTOT's 24/7 helpline on **01701 811397** and say you're a customer of an ABTOT-protected company. Read the [2018 Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukdsi/2018/9780111168479/contents) and more about [ABTOT](https://www.abtot.com/).
