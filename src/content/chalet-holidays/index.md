## Still independent, still the same people

Skis now come in all shapes and sizes, and Le Ski is still run by the same people — although they, too, are a slightly different shape nowadays.

Forty-odd seasons of experience proved invaluable when Brexit and Covid hit. Brexit meant totally changing how we staff our chalets, and we found a way to keep our individually tailored service across all our chalets. Many competitors didn't cope. Today, very few chalet operators still offer proper catering with their own flights and transfers — and fewer still are fully bonded, which is crucial when choosing who to book with.

We're intensely independent because that's how we like it, and that's how our guests are happiest.

## How we keep guests happy

We go on holiday too, so we notice what works and what doesn't. We keep what works and tweak what doesn't. We listen to guests and keep improving, because service really matters.

We've also kept things simple: we specialise in catered chalet holidays, in just four snowsure resorts.

## What we stand for

### We're helpful

Ring us on 01484 548996. Our UK team have worked for Le Ski for an impressive 184 years between them, so they'll very likely know the answer. And when you call, a human picks up — no options, no call centre.

### We're genuine

No hidden charges and no different service levels. Every chalet offers the same food, wine and care, so price differences simply reflect each chalet's position and standard.

### We're accommodating

Tell us what you're after and we'll see how we can help. Just ask.
