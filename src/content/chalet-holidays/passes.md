## How it works

Call 01484 548996 and our team will work out which passes suit your group. You can book and pay for passes **up to 10 days before departure**, so you're not paying for them before you need to. Got a free pass through an early-booking offer? You don't need to do anything.

Passes are electronic cards — keep yours in a zipped pocket on your **left** side and the barriers will read it automatically.

::: callout
**Don't leave it too late.** After the 10-day cut-off you'll need to buy passes at the resort cash desk — leaving the warm chalet, the Champagne and the canapés to queue in the cold, at whatever exchange rate they choose.
:::

## Prices for six days — Val d'Isère & Tignes

| Pass | Adult (19–64) | Child (8–18) & Senior (65–74) |
|---|---|---|
| Tignes–Val d'Isère (90 lifts) | £411 | £332 |
| Pedestrian | £71 (14–64) | £59 (5–13 and 65–74) |

In Tignes the seventh day is free. A 6–7 day pass gives two free entries to Val d'Isère's Aquasportif, and unlimited entry to Tignes' Le Lagon pool.

## Family passes

- **Courchevel / 3 Vallées** — one or two adults plus up to six children aged 5–17 (minimum three people).
- **Tignes–Val d'Isère** — families of four to seven with at least two adults and two children aged 8–18. Individual prices apply, then £290 comes off the total.

## Free passes and age limits

| | Free for | Discounted |
|---|---|---|
| Les 3 Vallées | Under 5 | Over 74 |
| Tignes–Val d'Isère | Under 8, and 75+ (congratulations!) | 65–74 |

We're required to check dates of birth for discounted and free passes, so please don't be offended if we ask. If the wrong category was ordered, we can't release the pass until the difference is paid — the lift company fines us otherwise.

## Early and late season

There may be cheaper rates for the weeks of 6 December and 11 April in Tignes and Val d'Isère when you book through us. Call 01484 548996 for details.

## Carré Neige

Carré Neige is cheap, convenient insurance that gets you off the mountain without any hassle, including off-piste reached by lifts. We can usually add it to passes bought through us — last season it was £19 for six days and £22 for seven. 2026/27 prices to follow.
