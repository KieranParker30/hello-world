## Courchevel & La Tania

| Activity | Details |
|---|---|
| **Tobogganing** | *Moriond Racing* (1650): a fast 3km track through trees and tunnels from the top of Ariondaz. A free track runs from the top of the Grangettes gondola to 1550, day and evening — rent a toboggan from most ski shops. Safe zones for 3–10s at the top of Tovets (1850) and in La Tania's ZenZone. |
| **Courchevel Aventure** | Piste-basher driving, husky sledding, snow scooters and fat-bikes. +33 4 79 22 16 71 |
| **Paragliding** | From Saulire or the Col de la Loze. FBI Parapente +33 1 76 35 00 87 · TandemTop +33 6 05 00 45 67 |
| **Snowshoeing** | Raquette Evasion (via Intersport) +33 6 80 33 56 11 · Simply Savoie — Mark, an International Mountain Leader, will meet you at the chalet: +33 6 33 44 20 41 |
| **Hot-air balloon** | Skivol, 20 years' experience. +33 6 83 97 53 26 |
| **Helicopter flights** | Panoramic flights from the 1850 Altiport — ask your Guest Services Manager |
| **[Aquamotion](aquamotion)** | Europe's largest mountain aqua centre: pools, slides, surf simulator, spa, climbing wall and gym. +33 9 71 00 73 00 |
| **Bowling** | Le Forum, 1850 (by La Croisette bus stop), 11am–3am. +33 4 79 08 23 83 |
| **Ice-skating** | Le Forum indoor rink, 1850, 3–7pm daily (until 10pm Wednesdays). +33 4 79 08 19 50 |
| **Climbing** | Indoor walls at Aquamotion and Le Forum |
| **Cinema** | Le Tremplin — look for "VO" or "VOST" for English. +33 9 60 47 74 62 |
| **Spa & massage** | Wellness packages at Aquamotion |

More at [courchevel.com](https://www.courchevel.com/en).

## Val d'Isère

| Activity | Details |
|---|---|
| **Evolution 2** | Husky rides, quad biking, ice climbing, ice diving and paragliding. +33 6 59 24 00 02 |
| **Paragliding** | Tandem flights from Solaise or Bellevarde, about €100. Weather permitting. |
| **Snowshoeing** | Guided by Raquettes Val d'Isère — spot the wildlife. +33 6 62 45 89 94 |
| **Heli-skiing** | A day in Italy, or reverse heli-skiing locally — ask your Guest Services Manager |
| **Ice karting & ice driving** | Including a driving simulator. Book ahead. +33 6 23 04 46 58 |
| **Sledging** | Free area beside the Savonette nursery lift, 10am–5pm. Hire sledges in the village. |
| **Ice-skating** | Village rink, 10am–12pm and 5–8pm. Adults €8, children €7. +33 4 79 22 82 05 |
| **Cinema** | English and subtitled films ("VO"/"VOST"). +33 4 79 06 03 43 |
| **Aquasportif** | Pool under the Olympic station — two free entries with a 2–15 day lift pass. +33 4 79 04 26 01 |

More at [valdisere.com](https://www.valdisere.com).
