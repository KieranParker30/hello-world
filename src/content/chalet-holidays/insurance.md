## Winter sports cover

Make sure your policy covers every activity you're planning, including off-piste if you'll venture there. We can't be responsible for extra costs in resort if your cover is inadequate. Many skiers travel with no insurance or the wrong policy — don't be one of them.

## GHIC — free

The UK Global Health Insurance Card has replaced the EHIC. It's free and gives you state healthcare in the EU, but it's not a substitute for travel insurance. [Apply on the NHS website](https://www.nhs.uk/using-the-nhs/healthcare-abroad/apply-for-a-free-uk-global-health-insurance-card-ghic/).

## Carré Neige

Cheap, convenient cover that gets you off the mountain without any hassle, including some lift-accessed off-piste. We can usually add it to passes bought through us — last season it was £19 for six days and £22 for seven; 2026/27 prices to follow. It can't be added to passes from the Free Ski Pass Offer, but you can add it in resort.
