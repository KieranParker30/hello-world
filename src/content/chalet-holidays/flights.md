## Why Sunday, why Chambéry?

Chambéry is the closest airport to our resorts, and Sunday is the quietest day to travel. We fly with **Jet2**, who have an excellent reputation for customer service; catering on board is buy-on-board.

Because we charter a fixed number of seats to match our chalet beds, flights are limited. We sometimes buy extra seats if we run out, but it isn't guaranteed — so book early to secure the flights you want.

## Baggage allowance

Set by the airline, per person:

| Item | Allowance |
|---|---|
| Hold bag | 1 bag of 22kg |
| Hand baggage | 1 bag of 10kg, max 56 × 45 × 25cm. Ski boots can be your one item if they fit. |
| Ski carriage | One set of skis and poles, or one snowboard (max 20kg). Boots go in hold or hand luggage. |
| Helmets | Within your hand-luggage allowance |
| Infants | Pushchair, travel cot or car seat, plus 10kg hold allowance |

## Ski carriage

Each set of skis or snowboard costs **£60 return if pre-booked**. Airlines limit how many they carry, so tell us when you book. At the airport it costs more, and there's no guarantee the airline will accept it.

## Travelling independently

Prefer to drive, or fly another way? No problem. There's usually a **£100 per person reduction** on full-price bookings (up to £50 on reduced-price holidays) — please check when booking.

We'll send you the chalet address and What3Words location, and our recommended route. If driving in France you must carry a warning triangle, hi-vis vest and breathalyser kit, and snow tyres or chains are compulsory in the French Alps in winter.

## The EU Entry/Exit System (EES)

The EU now registers visitors from outside the Schengen area with fingerprints and a photo (photo only for children under 12). Since April 2026 all airport arrivals are registered. The separate ETIAS visa-waiver scheme is due in late 2026 — we'll post details here as soon as they're confirmed. See the EU's [Travel Europe FAQs](https://travel-europe.europa.eu/ees_en).
