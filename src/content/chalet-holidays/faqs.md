## Flights & travel

??? Is food served on the flight?
No — catering on our flights is buy-on-board.

??? What are the flight times?
See [Flights & transfers](chalet-holidays/flights) for times from Gatwick and Manchester.

??? What happens when we land?
Look out for Le Ski's airport team, who'll show you to your transfer coach once you've collected your bags.

??? Which UK terminals do you use?
Gatwick South Terminal and Manchester Terminal 2.

??? Can you recommend airport parking?
We recommend APH — mention Le Ski for special rates.

??? Is ski carriage included?
Free ski carriage is sadly a thing of the past, but ours is much cheaper than most airlines: £60 per set of skis or board if booked in advance. At the airport it costs more, and only if the airline accepts it.

??? What's the luggage allowance, and does it include ski boots?
One 22kg hold bag and one 10kg hand bag each. Boots can be your hand-luggage item if they fit. Full details on [Flights & transfers](chalet-holidays/flights#baggage-allowance).

## In resort

??? How do I find out what's on?
Ask your Guest Services Manager or host. There's a resort information file in every chalet with restaurants, bus timetables and more — worth a read over afternoon tea.

??? What's for dinner?
Ask your host in the morning, so you can plan your lunch.

??? Can I book ski lessons through Le Ski?
Your Guest Services Manager can point you in the right direction. See our [ski school recommendations](chalet-holidays/lessons).

??? Is there childcare in resort?
We recommend nanny services in all four resorts — see [family holidays](chalet-holidays/family) or call 01484 548996.

??? Where are your chalets?
In Courchevel 1650 (Moriond), Courchevel La Tania, Val d'Isère and Tignes. See our [village maps](chalet-holidays/resort-maps).

??? How do I order lift passes?
Add them to your booking up to 10 days before departure — you'll pay in pounds and avoid bank charges abroad. See [lift passes](chalet-holidays/passes).

??? What are empty bed charges?
If a room isn't fully occupied, each empty bed is charged at 50% of the standard price. There's a limit per chalet, and we may need to amend bookings with too many. Call 01484 548996 with any questions.

## Packing

??? Do we need towels?
No — hand and bath towels are provided and changed midweek. Save your allowance for something we don't provide.

??? Is there a TV?
Some chalets have smart TVs. Where they don't, we keep a supply of TVs and DVD players in each resort, first come first served — ask your host or reserve through our UK office. There's a stock of novels to borrow, too.

??? Do I need an EU adaptor?
Yes, one. We provide a three-way UK extension lead in every bedroom, so a single adaptor does the job.

??? Do we need a hairdryer?
No — every bedroom has one.

??? What about pillows and blankets?
Both are provided, with spares in resort — just ask. If you have a favourite pillow, bring it.

??? What do I need for the hot tub?
Just a swimming costume. Towels, robes and slippers are provided.
