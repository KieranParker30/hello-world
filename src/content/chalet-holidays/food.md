## Breakfast

A proper breakfast matters before a day on the hill, and ours have evolved into something guests write home about. There's a continental spread, fresh crusty baguette and porridge, and a full English cooked to order on each of the five catered mornings.

No compromises: Heinz beans and ketchup, HP sauce and real British teabags. Breakfast is served from 8 to 9am.

## Afternoon tea

Come back from the slopes to a cake or biscuits freshly baked by your host, with baguette, jams, tea and coffee. On your host's days off we lay it out for you to help yourselves.

![Afternoon tea in a Le Ski chalet](Chocolate_cake_and_tea.jpg|Helens_cup_cakes.jpg)

## Champagne & canapés

Canapés and fizz are served at 7.30pm as you wind down for the evening. Our house Champagne is **Drappier** — we know the family, one of the most respected in Champagne, who make it with environmentally aware methods. It's served on your first and last nights.

On the other evenings we pour a Méthode Champenoise made by our friend **Nicolas Perret**, who lives just down the valley and makes our house wines too. Minimum wine miles, maximum quality.

## Dinner

Dinner is informal — this is your home for the week. Your host prepares and serves three courses using local producers and suppliers, and has been trained to keep food waste to a minimum. Afterwards, leave the table and relax with coffee and chocolates, if you have room.

![Dinner being served in a Le Ski chalet](c-Jonty-Wilde_LeSki_12.12.22-1674_1.jpg)

## Children

We're happy to serve an early dinner for little ones worn out after a day on the snow, with home-made, nutritious food they'll actually eat. Let us know before you travel and we'll brief your host.

## Special diets

If there's anything you'd rather not eat — for moral, medical or simply sensory reasons — tell us before you travel. We never charge extra for genuine dietary needs. See [Special diets](chalet-holidays/special-diets) for what we can source in the Alps.

## Wine

We only serve French wine, to keep wine miles down, and pour it unlimited with dinner — from bottles, not boxes. Last winter's house wines were:

| Whites | Rosés | Reds |

| IGP Sauvignon "Grand Terroir" | IGP Terres du Midi "Esprit des Sommets" | IGP Grenache "Grand Terroir" |
| IGP Chardonnay "Grand Terroir" | IGP Cinsault "Grand Terroir" | AOP Côtes du Rhône "Partides" |

We review the list every autumn. Fancy something special? Our [Wine Lovers' Package](chalet-holidays/wine) starts at £75.

## Your host's days off

Your host has two days off a week, usually Tuesday and Friday. We lay out a continental breakfast and afternoon tea, and it's the perfect excuse to try the local restaurants — our Guest Services Manager will book a table. Read our [restaurant guide](chalet-holidays/restaurants).
