## Party week

From 26 March to 2 April 2023, our Alpine teams organised two fabulous parties, in Courchevel and Val d'Isère. We ate, we drank, we danced, we sang — and we celebrated 40 years of Le Ski in the French Alps. Thank you to everyone who joined us.

## Our history

Le Ski was founded in 1982, and we've run catered chalet holidays in the Alps ever since. We've seen good times and bad, and with a little luck and a lot of hard work we're still here — thriving, in fact, with early bookings stronger than ever.

| Year | |
|---|---|
| 1982 | First guests arrive in Courchevel Moriond |
| 1992 | Our first chalets in Val d'Isère |
| 2015 | We start donating an annual holiday to Snow-Camp |
| 2017 | Voted "UK's Best Chalet Operator" in the Telegraph World Snow Awards |
| 2023 | 40th-anniversary parties in Courchevel and Val d'Isère |
| 2025 | Tignes becomes our fourth resort — our first new resort in 28 years |
| 2026 | Our 43rd season, and nominated Best Ski Operator |

We've welcomed many tens of thousands of guests, looked after by what we think are the friendliest, most genuine and most capable staff in the Alps. Thank you to everyone who's played a part in this remarkable adventure — which is very much still going.
