> Ever since finding out at 12 that working a ski season was a thing, I'd wanted to work for Le Ski. I wasn't disappointed. It was the third, and by far the best, chalet company I've worked for. The training was thorough and the management outstanding.
> — Rachael, Chalet Host, Courchevel

> Coming out in January I was a bit apprehensive, but everyone was incredibly welcoming and I felt at home right away. You'll hardly ski the same run twice!
> — Lara, Chalet Host, La Tania

> After four years in London I needed a mountain fix. Very quickly I felt like a valued member of the Le Ski family.
> — Gayle, Guest Services Manager, Val d'Isère

> Five months in France seemed daunting, but I've made friends for life. Le Ski has such a family feel — all the staff are looked after and appreciated.
> — Jennifer, Chalet Assistant, La Tania

> One of the most life-affirming decisions I've ever made. In return for hard graft, we were given the freedom to ski six days a week. I can't wait to do it all again.
> — Elin, Guest Services, Val d'Isère

> I've worked for Le Ski in La Tania for four winters. They really care for you and value the hard work you put in. I can't think of a better way to spend the winter than at the top of a mountain.
> — Molly, Chalet Manager, La Tania
