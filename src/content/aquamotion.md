## Aquamotion pass

- 32°C indoor and outdoor lagoon
- 25m swimming pool
- Diving pool with 1m and 3m boards
- 45m four-lane aqua slide and 110m rapid river canyon slide
- Children's and toddlers' play areas

## Aquawellness pass (adults only)

Everything above, plus:

- Floating salt pool
- Steam rooms and saunas
- Indoor and outdoor lagoons with jacuzzis and massage showers

![Aquamotion's indoor pools](AL_-_25133009-Aquafitness_rouge_barres_hydrotones.jpg)

## Prices

With so many options, pricing is best checked on [Aquamotion's website](https://www.aquamotion-courchevel.com). **Le Ski guests get a discount** when booking online — ask our team in resort for details.

## Getting there

Aquamotion is at most a ten-minute downhill stroll from the centre of Courchevel Moriond (1650), and under five minutes from several of our chalets. Tired legs? The free ski bus links La Tania, Le Praz, Courchevel Village (1550), Moriond (1650) and 1850.

## Also at Aquamotion

- Climbing wall
- Café
- Massage and beauty treatments
- Gym
