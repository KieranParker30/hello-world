The building is beautifully built into the landscape. Inside it's wonderfully spacious, with a great wall of glass that floods it with light and gives fantastic views across the valley while you swim.

## Where is it?

On the bend of the road down from Courchevel Moriond (1650), just before the turning for Courchevel Village (1550). It's a five-minute walk from our chalets Bisolet, Notus and Premier de Cordée, and under ten minutes from the rest of our Courchevel chalets.

Buses run every ten minutes from Moriond. From La Tania, buses run hourly at ten past the hour until 19.10, returning at 52 minutes past until 18.52, then at 20.22 and 22.22. Get off at the car park — an entrance takes you down a level and under the road, so there's no need to cross.

## Espace Aqualudique

A 25m pool, kept cool for proper lengths (swimmers: there's no pace clock or kit, so bring your own). A large, warmer leisure pool with water jets. Above them, the Pentagliss slide and the Canyon — great fun, but watch the shallow run-out on the Pentagliss and the tight turns in the Canyon. There's a deep diving pool with two heights, and outside, more jets, a mini lazy river and underwater bubble loungers. Little ones have a colourful area all to themselves.

## Espace Wellness (over 18s)

A "salty cave" you descend into — dark, with mood lighting and projections (we watched the northern lights) — where the salinity lets you float effortlessly. The main pool is half indoors, half out, with bubble beds and jets. Outside you'll find a sauna, steam room and cold plunge pool — if that doesn't wake you up after a day's skiing, nothing will.

## Spa & balnéo

Treatment rooms, a beauty salon and a hairdresser. A morning "awakening" session is €141, with two hours in the wellness area, a seaweed wrap, Vichy shower and facial. A full day is €165, adding a head massage and all-day access. Individual treatments start at €42.

## Indoor surf wave

Two settings: a flatter one for wakeboard-style riding with a rope, and a steeper one for surfing. Beginners start on a bodyboard and progress until they're standing — and trying 360s. Two people can ride at once, and the landing is soft. All you'll hurt is your pride.

## And more

- **Aqua classes** — aquabike, aquafusion, aquaboxing and aquafitness.
- **Climbing** — a great wall with a children's area upstairs (minimum 20kg), plus a bouldering cave.
- **Café Nikki** — when all that exercise has built up an appetite.
